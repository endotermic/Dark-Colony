~2Human campaign introduction:

~4Decades ago humans have started a colonization of Mars, totally successful despite meeting the aggressively-minded Tarr race. Numerous battles between aliens and three large human corporations - Stratus, Pan Luma and Aerogen - lead to the devastation of Tarr race. After the struggle had ended, Stratus and Pan Luma joined and created the Globo Corporation. Aerogen became a property of Marrus, one of the richest humans on Mars, the owner of Petra-7 refining concern. As the years have passed, humans have forgotten about the fearful events that had happened on Mars. In their minds its former inhabitants, the Taar, didn't exist anymore. Since now...

AD 2175. The archeologist camp Octava have sent an emergency signal. The rescue team, that was sent there. found the base completely destroyed and the staff slaughtered. It seems that the Tarr are back.
