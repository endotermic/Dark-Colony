~2Tarr campaign introduction:

~4Mars, the desired new home of Tarr have turned out to be a place of a bloodshed and suffering. Endless fights against the humans for domination weren't decisive, and they turned into meaningless and pointless struggle. The High Council, led by Ziru, decided to put an end to the fighting in order to spare the lives of our brothers. The Tarr moved into an underground tunnel complex, which was the relic of the unknown ancient civilization. Many of the Tarr refused to do so. One of them was Sirmu, the other High Council Member. Sirmu and his followers were observing humans' excavations near their base Octava, till the discovery of the Dalgar sanctuary- an ancient artifact of a great power. That was what they were waiting for...
